We show that $$\{\phi, \neg \phi\} \vdash \psi$$:

1. $$\phi \rightarrow \neg \phi \rightarrow \psi$$ (Axiom: Tautology)
2. $$\phi$$ (Assumption)
3. $$\neg \phi \rightarrow \psi$$ (Modus Ponens on 1, 2)
4. $$\neg \phi$$ (Assumption)
5. $$\psi$$ (Modus Ponens on 3, 4)
