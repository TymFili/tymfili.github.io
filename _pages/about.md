Hi, welcome to my website!

I am an undergraduate student studying Mathematics and Computer Science. My primary interests are in mathematical logic, discrete mathematics, and algorithms.

I use this website for:
1. **[Blog](/blog/):** Short posts about whatever might interest me.
2. **[Teaching](/teaching/):** Lecture notes, problem sheets, and study guides for students and peers.
3. **[Theorems & Proofs](/theorems/):** A curated reference library of formal mathematical proofs.
4. **[Definitions](/definitions/):** A formal dictionary of mathematical logic, order theory, and set theory.
